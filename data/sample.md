# Study Notes: Electricity

These are my short notes about how electricity works. I wrote them for the physics revision session.

## Current and Voltage

Electric current is the flow of electric charge through a wire, measured in amperes. Voltage is the push that makes the charges move, measured in volts. Think of water in a pipe: the flow is like current and the water pressure is like voltage.

### Ohm's Law

Ohm's law connects all three quantities: voltage equals current multiplied by resistance. If the resistance goes up and the voltage stays the same, the current becomes smaller.

## Power

Electrical power is measured in watts. A bulb that uses 60 watts uses 60 joules of energy every second. The electricity bill depends on how many watts a device uses and how many hours it stays switched on.

## Safety

Fuses and circuit breakers protect a home. If too much current flows, the fuse wire melts and the circuit is broken, which stops the wire from getting hot enough to start a fire.
